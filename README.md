# Zimbabwe Open Surveys (zim-surveys)

This repository is the **parent hub for open surveys focused on Zimbabwe**. It brings together multiple community-driven surveys across different domains, with the goal of collecting, publishing, and maintaining **open, anonymized datasets** that anyone can use.

The project is built on the belief that **access to data empowers better decisions**, stronger communities, and more transparent systems.

---

## 🎯 Mission

The mission of **zim-surveys** is to:

* Collect high-quality, anonymous survey data relevant to Zimbabwe
* Publish survey results as **open datasets**
* Support research, innovation, policy discussions, and public insight
* Encourage data-driven conversations across industries

All surveys under this repository follow principles of **openness, anonymity, and community benefit**.

---

## 📚 Surveys in This Repository

Each survey lives in its own sub-repository or folder and includes its own documentation.

Current and planned surveys include:

* **Zimbabwe Tech Industry Survey**
  Insights into education, skills, tools, AI usage, and employment trends in the tech sector.

* **Future Surveys**
  Additional surveys may cover areas such as education, labour markets, entrepreneurship, digital access, and community development.

---

## 🔍 What Makes This Project Different

* **Anonymous by design** – no personally identifiable information is collected
* **Open data** – results are shared publicly for reuse
* **Community-first** – surveys are created with local context in mind
* **Vendor-neutral** – no commercial agenda tied to responses

---

## 📊 Data Transparency & Usage

For each completed survey:

* Raw responses are cleaned and anonymized
* Datasets are published in open formats (CSV / JSON)
* Documentation is provided to explain fields and limitations

The data may be used for:

* Academic and independent research
* Journalism and storytelling
* Product and startup validation
* Policy and advocacy work

Users are encouraged to interpret and share findings responsibly.
